About Me

I am an M.S. student in Transportation at the College of Transport & Communications, Shanghai Maritime University, where I also received my B.S. in Transportation Management in 2025. My research interests include complex networks, multimodal transport systems, and maritime transport policy.

My recent work applies network science and policy evaluation methods to multimodal transport, including a PMC index analysis of China’s multimodal transport policies published in Regional Studies in Marine Science (2026). I have also interned at the Shanghai Shipping Exchange (Market Department), working on liner freight rate filing review and shipping market data analysis.

Outside research, I enjoy fitness, basketball, and swimming.

I’m interested in research collaborations and internship opportunities. Feel free to reach out if you’d like to chat!

Research Projects

Unraveling China’s Multimodal Transport Policies: The PMC Index Perspective
SCI · Second Author

PMC index and network construction: participated in the quantitative evaluation of China’s multimodal transport policies from 2015 to 2023, covering policy text screening, keyword extraction, co-occurrence matrix construction, and complex network centrality analysis. Converted policy clauses into encodable and comparable indicators, and built a PMC index evaluation system with 9 first-level and 40 second-level variables to identify structural gaps in safeguard measures, low-carbon orientation, and information platform construction.

Port & Maritime Policy and Market Research Project

Maritime policy research: studied changes in international maritime conventions, conducted COSCO customer satisfaction surveys, and contributed to the compilation of a shipping agency textbook. Around green shipping rules, maritime regulations, customer experience, and freight forwarding processes, carried out policy review, case synthesis, feedback classification, and technical writing, drawing on knowledge of carriage of goods by sea, international shipping agency, bills of lading and document flow, and vessel operation compliance.
